<div class="page-header">
  <div class="wrap">
    <h1>How to Join</h1>
    <p>Northeast Aero RC Club has open membership. Guest pilots and the general public are welcome, and we offer pilot instruction for anyone new to the hobby. Here is how to get started.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<section>
  <div class="steps">
    <div class="step">
      <div class="step-num">1</div>
      <div class="step-body">
        <h3>Come out and visit</h3>
        <p>You do not need to be a member to stop by and watch. Come out to the field on Sheriff Fewell Road, meet some members, and see what the club flies.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div class="step">
      <div class="step-num">2</div>
      <div class="step-body">
        <h3>Get an AMA membership</h3>
        <p>AMA membership is required to fly at the field. You can find membership options on the Academy of Model Aeronautics site.</p>
        <p><a href="https://www.modelaircraft.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">AMA membership info</a></p>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div class="step">
      <div class="step-num">3</div>
      <div class="step-body">
        <h3>Reach out to the club</h3>
        <p>Message our Facebook group or contact club treasurer Bill Mahan to ask about joining, current dues, and any paperwork needed.</p>
        <p>
          <a href="{{ site.facebook_group }}" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Facebook group</a>
          &nbsp;or&nbsp;
          <a href="tel:{{ site.contact.phone | replace: ' ', '' }}">{{ site.contact.phone }}</a>
          &nbsp;or&nbsp;
          <a href="mailto:{{ site.contact.email }}">{{ site.contact.email }}</a>
        </p>
      </div>
    </div>
    <div class="step">
      <div class="step-num">4</div>
      <div class="step-body">
        <h3>Come to a club meeting</h3>
        <p>We hold regular club meetings where you can meet the rest of the members and our club leaders. See the <a href="{{ '/leaders/' | relative_url }}">leaders page</a> to know who to look for.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="fact-card" style="margin-top: 30px;">
    <h3>One rule at the field</h3>
    <p>No drugs and no alcohol.</p>
  </div>
</section>
